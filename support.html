How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose U.S. Citizenship Test Prep and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next month.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is available as an auto-renewable monthly subscription (US$2.99 per month) or a one-time Lifetime purchase (US$9.99) in the U.S. App Store. It unlocks Mock Exams 8 to 20, removes ads and shows full statistics. Prices in other countries may differ.

Why does the free version show ads?

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob on screens outside the exams. On first launch you may be asked for consent where required, and whether to allow tracking; you can decline and still use the app with non-personalized ads. Premium removes all ads. You can change your tracking choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

Your exam history, notes and flags are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Are the questions official?

No. The questions are practice questions written for study and based on facts in the free USCIS study materials. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by USCIS or the U.S. government, and it cannot guarantee a passing result. See uscis.gov for the most current information.
