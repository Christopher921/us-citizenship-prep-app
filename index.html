U.S. Citizenship Test Prep

Practice for the U.S. naturalization civics test (2025 version) with 20 mock exams of 20 questions each, oral interview practice, a 12-of-20 practice benchmark, personal notes, flagged questions, answer review and progress statistics.

Mock Exams 1 to 7 are free. The free version shows ads. A monthly Premium subscription removes ads, unlocks Mock Exams 8 to 20, and gives full access to exam review, incorrect and flagged questions, notes and statistics (free users see a preview of Review and Statistics).
